# Evaluator feedback

Answer from what you saw, not from what you think the right answer is. Blank and
"I could not tell" are useful answers.

Reference while answering: `summary.md`, written beside this file in the
generated `evaluator-artifacts/` pack, labels the approved boundary, the
observed boundary, the material change between them, the exact held call, your
operator decision, and what receipt verification proves.
"The held call" is the one call this run sent through Interlock's gateway that
was stopped. The "Held call" section of `summary.md` names it and numbers it.

1. What did you think Interlock was checking before you ran it?
2. What changed in the MCP tool?
3. Why was the call held?
4. What evidence supported the decision?
5. What would you do next: approve, reject, or rebaseline?
6. Where did the process confuse or slow you down?
7. Would you keep Interlock in this workflow? Why or why not?
